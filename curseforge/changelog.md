# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — Minecraft 26.1.2

First release.

- Added **Result Sentinel** — A handheld scanner. Right-click to list how many crops are ready to harvest and where the nearest one is; it also pings automatically while you hold it.
- Added **Growth Lamp** — A glowing block that bone-meals nearby crops over time. Radius, chance and speed are configurable.
- Added **Chicken Coop** — Collects dropped eggs and feathers in range. Right-click to empty it, and it can hatch a stored egg into a chick.
- Added **Animal Feeder** — Right-click with breeding food to store it, then it feeds nearby adult animals. Right-click empty-handed to take the food back.
- Added **Seed Pouch** — Right-click farmland to plant a plane of seeds from your inventory in one go.
- Added **XP Siphon** — Right-click a furnace to collect the experience it has stored up.
- Shared HUD design system for consistent panels across every module.
- In-game configuration screen with full English localisation.
- 30 automated calculation regression checks.
